Shivani Jha

Shayad aap mujhe ignore kar rahi hain...

Aur sach kahu to main deserve karta hoon.

Main baar-baar wahi moment yaad kar raha hoon...

Jahan maine aapko hurt kiya.

Us waqt shayad mujhe samajh nahi aaya...

Par ab samajh aa raha hai.

Aap mere liye sirf ek insaan nahi hain...

Aap meri aadat hain.

Jab aap baat nahi karti...

To sab kuch thoda sa khaali lagta hai.

Main perfect nahi hoon...

Par aapko khona bhi nahi chahta.

Main change hona chahta hoon...

Sirf bolne ke liye nahi, sach me.

Main aapko force nahi karunga...

Par ek chance zaroor maangunga.

Kya aap ek chance dengi?
